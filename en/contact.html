Who we are

FERCOMEX-TCN is a non-governmental development organisation (ONGD) incorporated under Congolese law, active since 1997 in Haut-Katanga province.

We correspond in French, English and Swahili.

Support our work

Every contribution directly strengthens food security, farmer training and environmental protection in the communities we serve. To make a donation or become a partner, contact us and we will share the secure procedure.
