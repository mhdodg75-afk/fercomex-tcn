Who we are

A farm that grew into a movement

FERCOMEX-TCN (Ferme Communautaire et Expérimentale – La Terre de Chez-Nous) is a Congolese non-governmental organisation founded in 1997. It began as an experimental farm serving smallholder farmers and has grown into a local development actor that strengthens the capacity of rural communities, improves food security and promotes sustainable farming practices.

Today we also work on climate resilience, sustainable waste management and ecological solutions for inclusive development, while taking part in international initiatives for environmental justice.

Our story and mission

Since 1997, FERCOMEX-TCN has worked alongside the communities of Haut-Katanga: nearly thirty years of continuous presence in the field, four areas of work and a community farm at Kaisala serving smallholder farmers.
