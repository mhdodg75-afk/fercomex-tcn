Our work in the field

Four main areas of intervention serving communities and the environment.

Area 1

Sustainable community agriculture

We train and support smallholder farmers in adopting sustainable agricultural practices to improve productivity and community food security.

  • Technical training for smallholder farmers
  • Support to community organisations
  • Farmer field schools and experimental plots
  • Lasting improvements to food security
Area 2

Climate resilience

We strengthen communities’ capacity to adapt to the effects of climate change through nature-based solutions and local adaptation practices.

  • Nature-based solutions
  • Climate-adapted farming practices
  • Awareness raising on climate issues
  • Local adaptation initiatives
Area 3

Environment and waste management

We preserve natural resources and promote environmental protection through concrete field action and awareness raising.

  • Reforestation and restoration of degraded ecosystems
  • Sustainable waste management
  • Promotion of the circular economy
  • Ecosystem conservation

Area 4

Empowering women and youth

We foster the economic empowerment of women, youth and vulnerable groups through income-generating activities and green entrepreneurship.

  • Income-generating activities
  • Green entrepreneurship
  • Training and support for vulnerable groups
  • Fighting poverty
