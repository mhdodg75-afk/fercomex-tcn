Privacy policy

How we protect the information of visitors to this website.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is published by FERCOMEX-TCN (Ferme Communautaire et Expérimentale – La Terre de Chez-Nous), a non-governmental organisation based in Lubumbashi, Democratic Republic of the Congo. For any question about your data: contact@fercomex-tcn.com.

2. Data we collect

This website only collects personal data when you use the contact form: your name, email address and the content of your message. This information is used solely to reply to you. It is never sold or shared with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Only technical cookies strictly necessary for the site to function may be set by our hosting provider. The site uses no third-party services: fonts, images and videos are hosted on the site itself, and no data is sent to any other company when you visit.

4. Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Messages sent through the form pass through a secure service and are kept only for as long as needed to handle your request.

5. Your rights

You may at any time request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to contact@fercomex-tcn.com.
